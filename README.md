# Baha Kırbaşoğlu

AI consultant at [PEAKUP](https://peakup.org), a Microsoft partner, and M.Sc. student in Computer Engineering at Istanbul Technical University.

At PEAKUP I take enterprise AI projects from discovery to handover: scoping, architecture, implementation, evaluation and documentation. Most of that work runs on Azure AI Foundry, Copilot Studio, Microsoft 365 Copilot and Power Platform. I also deliver corporate AI training and teach a university course through the Sektör Kampüste programme.

My graduate research is on small-lesion detection in FDG PET/CT imaging.

## Selected work

| Project | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| [Fraud-Detection](https://github.com/Bahakirbasogluu/Fraud-Detection) | One-class classification for fraud detection (OCSVM, GMM, Isolation Forest, LOF, OCAN) with voting and stacking ensembles, on credit card and Medicare data. |
| [Fraud-Detection-GT](https://github.com/Bahakirbasogluu/Fraud-Detection-GT) | The graph-based companion study on the same datasets: centrality and community analysis, spectral methods, and GNNs (GCN, GraphSAGE, GAT, DOMINANT). |

| [docgen_data](https://github.com/Bahakirbasogluu/docgen_data) | Turkish and English technical-support dialogue dataset (341 samples) for issue classification, title generation, sentiment analysis and summarization. |

Client work lives in private repositories. The older repositories on this profile are coursework from my undergraduate years.
